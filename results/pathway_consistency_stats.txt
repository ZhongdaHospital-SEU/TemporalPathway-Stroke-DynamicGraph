PATHWAY CROSS-COHORT CONSISTENCY AND STROKE-PROCESS ENRICHMENT
==============================================================================

Atlas: 70 significant pathways (GSE37587 subacute, paired
       Baseline -> Follow-Up within n=34 stroke patients, FDR < 0.05).
Background: all 12614 atlas tests (GSE37587 subacute n=6308 +
            GSE58294 acute n=6306, paired 3h -> 24h within n=23 patients).

TASK 1 - CROSS-COHORT DIRECTION CONSISTENCY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

What each cohort supports (no overclaiming):
  - GSE16561_stroke_vs_control             [disease_vs_control] Whole-blood stroke (n=39) vs control (n=24); cross-sectional disease-state association, no time information available.
  - GSE22255_stroke_vs_control             [disease_vs_control] Whole-blood stroke (n=20) vs control (n=20), acute phase; cross-sectional disease-state association.
  - GSE58294_stroke_vs_control_3h          [disease_vs_control] Cardioembolic stroke (n=23) vs control (n=23) at 3h; cross-sectional disease-state association at an acute timepoint.
  - GSE58294_stroke_vs_control_5h          [disease_vs_control] Cardioembolic stroke (n=23) vs control (n=23) at 5h; cross-sectional disease-state association at an acute timepoint.
  - GSE58294_stroke_vs_control_24h         [disease_vs_control] Cardioembolic stroke (n=23) vs control (n=23) at 24h; cross-sectional disease-state association at an acute timepoint.
  - GSE58294_24h_vs_3h                     [temporal_paired] Paired 3h -> 24h within cardioembolic stroke patients (n=23); direct temporal replication of the atlas acute-phase comparison.
  - GSE37587 is the atlas cohort itself (the 70 were selected there);
    it is not used as a replication cohort.

Concordance = fraction of atlas pathways whose direction (up/down)
matches the direction computed in the comparison cohort.
Binomial sign test: two-sided exact test vs P = 0.5.

  - Background for GSE58294_24h_vs_3h excludes the 6,306 GSE58294 acute
    atlas rows (the same paired comparison would be trivially concordant),
    leaving the 6,308 GSE37587 rows as the independent background; 6,293 of

    control; for temporal comparisons 'up' means higher at the later time.
  - fisherP = two-sided Fisher exact test comparing concordance among the
    70 atlas pathways vs the background concordance rate.

(coagulation > hypoxia/ischemia > inflammation/immune > neurogenesis/
plasticity > metabolism > apoptosis > other).

BH-FDR across 7 categories; OR with Haldane-Anscombe 0.5 correction.
